# 3D Home Tour: Plot 54(S) & 55

- `index.html`: open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. It needs an internet connection the first time, to load Three.js from jsDelivr.
  - **Walk**: click to look around, **WASD** or the arrow keys to move, **Shift** to run, **V** to switch views.
  - **Dollhouse**: roof off. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
  - **Style**: Modern / Scandinavian / Warm Luxury.
  - **Time**: 6 AM to 10 PM, using a real November sun path for Madurai. Lamps turn on at dusk.
  - **Start Tour**: a guided walk through every room with room cards and generated music (use the Mute button to silence it). It ends by rising into the dollhouse view.
- `home-tour.mp4`: a 1:49 recording of the guided tour (1280×720, 30 fps, with the generated soundtrack). It goes from morning to golden hour and ends in a night dollhouse view.
- `PLAN.md`: the reading of the floor plan (rooms, doors, windows, heights and open questions) and the design plan for the walkthrough.
