## 5. Heights and walls

- **Wall height:** 10'-0" floor to ceiling, with a 7'-0" door and window head (main door 7'-6") and a 3'-0" typical sill.
- **Wall thickness:** 9" outer walls, 4½" partitions. The slab is 7" with a 3' parapet.
- **Staircase:** 18 risers of 6⅔" in two flights of 9 with a mid landing. It goes up to a **head room** (mumty) on the terrace, which is the "HEAD ROOM" line in the estimate.

## 6. Things that are unclear on the plan

1. **Scale:** the sheet is marked NTS, and the 39'-0" vs 39'-7½" side depths don't match. I used the written room sizes and the 29' × 38' building.
2. **Staircase:** there is no size, riser count or landing level. The "UP" arrow and tread lines suggest a dog-leg. The breakfast-counter stools are drawn inside the stair area, so the counter has been placed at the kitchen's north edge.
3. **Kitchen access:** no door is drawn between the Hall and the Kitchen. I treated it as open, reached past the foot of the stair.
4. **Foyer front:** the foyer's front wall and the porch below it are not clearly closed. I modelled the foyer as a semi-open sit-out with an opening onto a covered porch, reached through the wicket gate.
5. **"PVC" door:** the swing arc sits between the Pooja and the Store. I assumed it is the Store door from the Kitchen.
6. **Pooja:** no door is drawn, so it has an open carved arch facing the Hall.
7. **Bedroom 2:** it is 10' × 12' but continues under Toilet 2 as an L-shape. I used the extra space as a study nook.
8. **Labels "L", "O", "W.G":** L is read as a store louvre and W.G as a wicket gate. **O** (front right corner of the parking) is not identified. It could be a column, an electrical meter or an outlet, so it is left out.
9. **"EXTRA (AT TIME)" notes** are cost items in the estimate. They are not drawn elements.
10. **Floor finishes, false ceiling, ceiling height and plinth height** are not given. I used a 10' ceiling and a single floor level.
11. **Wardrobes:** the hatched blocks on both sides of the bedroom partition are read as built-in wardrobes.
12. The 8' compound wall sits only 3' from the south windows. It cuts off low morning and evening sun, so direct sun patches are strongest around midday.

**Lighting:**
- A real sun path for Madurai (9.9°N) in November, from 6 AM to 10 PM, casting soft PCF shadows through the windows. The light is warm and golden from 5 PM to 6 PM.
- At night the ceiling lights, bedside lamps, floor lamps, dining pendants, LED coves and the pooja lamp fade on with a soft glow.
- Floors and glass reflect a live capture of the actual interior.
