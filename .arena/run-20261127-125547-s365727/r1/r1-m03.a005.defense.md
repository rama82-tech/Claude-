ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Printed dimensions are face-to-face or outer-to-outer, not centreline, so the 2% test was ill-defined. Fixed: `dimensions[]` with an explicit `datum` (inner, outer, centre), Test 1 measures between the faces named by the datum, and the datum is asked for, never assumed.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The "reliable v1" was the upload path I called opt-in, and nothing helped a person read coordinates. Fixed: extraction now writes pixel coordinates (`plan_px.json`) that code converts to metres, a pixel-grid image makes hand correction possible, and the v1 includes the scale step. Privacy is stated honestly: Claude reading the image sends it to Anthropic, and a fully local manual mode is offered instead.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. file:// fetches, GLTFLoader being an addon, and missing SwiftShader flags are all real. Fixed: a localhost HTTP server, vendored three.js with an import map including the addons, explicit Chromium flags, a smoke test for WebGL, and a pure-Python matplotlib fallback for the top-down render.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. Round joins and caps would round outside corners and the bounding-box test would not notice. Fixed: square caps and mitre joins, plus a test that a rectangle room's footprint has exactly 4 outer vertices and area equals the analytic value.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. A unioned footprint loses wall identity and zero-height strips are degenerate. Fixed: each wall is its own solid with overlaps resolved by a deterministic priority (earlier walls win, later walls are clipped), openings split the wall along its length into segments, and zero-height pieces are omitted. Watertightness is tested per piece.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. Fixed: the mapping is stated (plan x, y to glTF x, 0, -y, z-up elevation to glTF y), a deliberately asymmetric fixture with a marker corner checks for mirroring, and the viewer exposes a projection function that calibrates the orthographic camera for the pixel-match test.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. Centroids can lie outside concave rooms. Fixed: use `representative_point` or the pole of inaccessibility, with a test that the camera is inside the room polygon and clear of walls by 0.3 m.
