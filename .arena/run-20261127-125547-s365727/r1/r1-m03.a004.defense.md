ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Real gap: stage 3 and stage 4 disagreed about door gaps. Fixed with an explicit "wall-run merge" step: wall segments keep their gaps, collinear segments are bridged into one continuous wall, and each bridged gap becomes an opening record. Polygonize then runs on the bridged walls, never on gap-split ones. Gaps wider than 3.5 m are not bridged and fail loudly.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The two-dimension gate contradicted Assumption 2 and the user-override path. Fixed with a tiered scale policy: user distance is authoritative, one printed dimension is accepted but marked single-source and needs overlay confirmation, two or more must agree within 5% (the same figure as the stage 6 check), and the door prior is only allowed with an explicit approximate flag.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. The plan never said which polygon the area and bounding-box checks use. Fixed by defining geometry conventions: walls are stored on the centreline, rooms carry a net (interior-face) polygon, printed areas are compared against net area, and the bounding-box check uses the wall footprint including thickness. The success test is restated on those definitions, with a dimension-reference option (face or centre) for printed lengths.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The v1 started from a hand-written JSON, so it did not turn any image into 3D. Fixed: v1 is now image or PDF in, GLB out. Claude vision proposes plan.json from the image (with a pixel grid drawn on it), a validator and overlay check it, and the user fixes errors. Vector PDFs get an extraction route in the same version. The hand-written JSON remains only as the first test fixture.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. The checks as written would flag valid plans. Fixed with a severity table: overlap is only an error for collinear duplicate walls, not at corners and T-junctions. Openings are checked against the usable length of a wall run (corner thickness excluded), and a door within 5 cm of a corner is a warning. Rooms under 1 m2 are a warning, and only a degenerate polygon under 0.25 m2 is an error.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. The fallback was unspecified and versions were not pinned. Fixed: a pinned primary (Blender 4.2 LTS tarball with its bundled Python, verified by a step-0 script, with engine names read from the installed enum) and a fully specified fallback (Shapely and trimesh, walls built as sill, header and pier pieces with no booleans, GLB plus Playwright screenshots). A table states what the fallback can and cannot produce, and the proxy and download risk is named.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. Centroid cameras and the 10-degree skew rejection both fail on common inputs. Fixed: cameras use Shapely polylabel (a point guaranteed inside the polygon) plus a clearance check. Rotation (any angle, including 90 degrees) is auto-corrected from the dominant angle modulo 90, and only genuine perspective (parallel-line families that converge) is rejected or sent to a homography. Diagonal-wall plans are flagged rather than misread.
