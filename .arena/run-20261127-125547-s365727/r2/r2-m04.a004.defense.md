ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Bridging every gap up to 3.5 m turned a missed wall into a silent arch. Fix: a gap becomes an opening only with positive evidence (swing arc, glazing lines, or a user-confirmed record). A gap without evidence is an "unresolved gap" error, and exterior gaps over 1.2 m always need confirmation. The 3.5 m rule is gone. Two silent-case tests were added.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The two origins really were mixed. Fix: `offset` is defined as the centreline coordinate of the opening's start, measured from the run's start point. The check uses the clear span [t0, t1], the centreline coordinates of the end walls' inner faces, with a worked example (door at 3.15 m with width 0.9 m is valid on a 4.2 m run with 0.2 m walls).
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Stage 3 threw away the thin glazing lines. Fix: stage 3 now writes a separate thin-stroke layer. Stage 4 looks in that layer inside every gap and inside continuous walls. A gap crossed by thin parallel lines is a window. Door, window and arch are no longer decided by width alone.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The v1 front end is vision-proposed, so the "never an AI guess" claim was overstated. Fix: the claim is reworded. Every wall carries a `source` field (user, vector, cv, vision). A vision wall must be snapped to measured line evidence within 15 px, or it is flagged "unsupported" and blocks the build until confirmed. The image is downscaled or tiled to the model's input size before the grid is drawn, and coordinates are mapped back.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. One global `dim_reference` is wrong for mixed dimension chains. Fix: each evidence entry records its own `ref` (face, centre, outer or unknown). Scale is fitted only from face-to-face or known-ref dimensions. Outer dimensions are used only as a check, after wall thickness is added once. Unknown ref widens the tolerance by thickness.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. The 5 percent tolerance conflicted with the 2 percent success test, and the 5 mm gate was circular. Fix: scale agreement is tiered (up to 2 percent accept, 2 to 5 percent accept only with overlay confirmation, over 5 percent stop). The 5 mm gate is renamed a builder self-check. A new fidelity check compares the top-down render's wall mask with the source image mask (IoU) and the model's overall dimension with an independent printed overall dimension, within 2 percent.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. Floors from `polygon_net` leave voids at thresholds and under walls. Fix: a slab is built from the union of room net polygons and wall footprints (0.1 m thick), and room floor finishes sit on top of the net polygons. A render test checks that no void shows at a doorway.
