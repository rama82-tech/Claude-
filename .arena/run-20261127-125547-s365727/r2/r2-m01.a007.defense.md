ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The fast path told Claude to read coordinates the plan says it cannot measure, and the 4 h figure ignored the listed steps. Fixed: the fast path now transcribes printed dimensions in metres (no pixel reading), a `grid.py` helper draws a labelled pixel grid for any click or read-off, and the time is restated at 5 to 6.5 h from the listed steps.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. Unpinned `npm pack three` pulls a build that imports `./three.core.js`, and flattening the files breaks the `../utils/` import. Fixed: pin `three@0.160.0`, copy whole directories keeping their relative paths, use import-map entries for `three` and `three/addons/`, and fail the test if the console shows any 404.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Flat caps do leave an L-corner notch of t/2 by t/2, and grid snapping does not fix it. Fixed: extend a wall end at an L-corner by half the thickness of the wall it meets, and add an exact area test, (W+t)(D+t) minus (W-t)(D-t) for a rectangle.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. Printed dimensions are inside-face or outside-face, not centreline, so check 2 raised false failures. Fixed: `plan.json` gets a `dims` list with a `basis` of inside, outside or centre, and the check measures face-to-face or outer extent from the built geometry.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. The thickness, style and kernel ordering was circular, and the closing kernel and the one-style-per-plan rule were wrong. Fixed: scale comes first, so thickness is bounded at 0.08 to 0.45 m in pixels. Style B no longer uses morphological closing: it uses LSD segments and the same pairing algorithm as the vector path. All detectors run, results are merged and colour-coded per wall, so mixed plans work, and single-line style is opt-in only.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. The cut box could reach into the adjoining wall's volume, and the per-wall fallback was impossible after the union. Fixed: the valid opening interval runs between the adjoining walls' faces, and check 4 uses it. Openings are now built per wall id with 2D polygon ops plus height bands (main wall, lintel, sill), with no 3D boolean needed.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. A single long axis fails on merged L-shaped or ring-shaped CAD fills, and furniture, columns and text can pass the width filter. Fixed: rectangular fills (area at least 0.9 of the minimum rotated rectangle) become one wall; other fills are rasterised at 1 cm per pixel and sent through the raster skeleton and thickness path. Compact blobs become columns, and text boxes from `get_text` are subtracted.
