ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The plan built Z-up and exported unrotated into a Y-up viewer. Fixed: build.py rotates -90 degrees about X before export (plan x,y,z becomes x,z,-y), and the camera specs are rewritten for Y-up, with a check that the GLB bounding box height equals wall_height.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The extractor and schema contradicted each other. Fixed: walls are continuous centre-lines through door and window gaps (collinear pieces are merged across gaps up to 3 m), the gaps are emitted as numbered "opening hints" with suggested wall, at and w, and the bridging-rectangle step is deleted. Rooms are the interior rings of the uncut wall union, so doors can never leak.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. --ref-face did nothing definite. Fixed: the two reference points are snapped to the nearest extracted wall-face line of the declared kind (outer, inner or centre), and scale is the real length divided by the snapped pixel distance. The grid is 25 px on zoomed crops, and vector PDFs avoid eyeballing completely.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The check was circular. Fixed: scale uses one printed dimension, and acceptance uses at least two other printed dimensions, held out and typed with --check, each within 2 percent (one threshold everywhere). The outer-box check is kept only as a sanity check and is no longer called an accuracy test. For a vector PDF with a stated drawing scale, the printed overall dimension is itself an independent check.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. Window and door symbols and furniture can pair up as fake walls. Fixed with explicit filters: minimum pair length 0.8 m, a pair must belong to a connected component touching the main outline, small components under 2 m total are dropped, and short perpendicular jamb lines are ignored. I also noted that window lines inside a wall gap are collinear with the wall, so they merge into it, which is correct. PyMuPDF "re", "qu" and polyline items are decomposed: thin filled rectangles become walls directly, and others are split into 4 segments or per-segment lines. A `delete` list in plan.json removes the remaining fakes after overlay review. I do not claim symbol-rich plans are fully automatic.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. The threshold was garbled. Fixed: a solid-wall result is accepted only if the wall union has a closed outer ring enclosing at least one interior ring of at least 4 square metres, with a total length not above 3 times the outer bounding box perimeter. Otherwise it retries with paired lines, then stops loudly. Statistics are always printed.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. The cutaway height was not a band edge, and the camera up vector was missing. Fixed: the cut height is added to the breakpoint list and bands above it are dropped. Top-down uses camera.up = (0,0,-1) so plan x runs right and plan y runs up the screen, matching overlay.png.
