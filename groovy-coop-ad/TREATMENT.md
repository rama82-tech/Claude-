# The Groovy Coop: 30 s ad, "Kinetic Tiles"

**Brief.** A 30 s, 16:9 ad for The Groovy Coop, a women-owned retro and vintage boutique in Historic
Downtown McKinney, TX. Pure motion graphics: a 16×9 grid of square cards that flip, ripple and land
on the beat. Music and sound effects only, no voice. The name is set in type (Bungee) as a placeholder
until the logo file arrives. The approved style frame (`styleframe.png`) is the end card.

**Look.** 80s Memphis colours on cream: hot pink, teal, sunflower, violet, tangerine, mint, deep-purple
ink for outlines and hard drop shadows. Patterns: zigzag, dots, squiggle, checker, terrazzo, leopard.
Product icons drawn in code from the shop's photos: rock tees, funny socks, crystals, candles,
records, cassettes, hangers, shades, a game controller, and a little hen for "Coop".

**Motion rules.** Every change is a card flip (0.36 s, about its vertical axis), landing exactly on a
beat or a sixteenth. Big moves are ripples ordered by distance from their cause. Nothing fades.
Things that live on their cards keep moving: the record spins, the sun turns.

## Three structures

1. **Wall → name → shelves → vibe → end card.** The grid fills, the name lands letter by letter,
   the wall turns into a shop shelf of four product cards, then the vibe (women-owned, everyone
   welcome, cozy stop on the square, community), then the end card.
2. **A day on the square.** Tiles act as a clock from opening to a block party at dusk. Stronger
   story, but it needs facts (hours, events) we don't have, and it delays the name.
3. **One tile, zoomed.** Start inside one sock tile and pull back through ever larger grids
   until the whole shop is a tile on the square. Elegant, but the name comes last and products
   read as small.

**Choice: 1.** An ad has to say the name early and often; structure 1 lands it at 4 s and again at
the end, shows four products large enough to read, and ends on the approved frame.

## Beats (120 BPM, 1 bar = 2 s)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–3.3 | Grid lines draw; pattern cards ripple in from the centre on sixteenths. Pad and arpeggio, filter opening |
| 3.6–9 | Drums drop at 4.0. G R O O V Y land one per beat, then THE, C, the record O, the sun O, P |
| 10–18 | The wall wipes left to right into a shelf: "What's in the coop?", then VINTAGE, ROCK TEES, FUNNY SOCKS, CRYSTALS on beats, then a strip of candles, records, collectibles, gifts |
| 18–24 | Vibe: women-owned, everyone welcome, a cozy stop on the square, the kids' retro-games corner, block parties and downtown events |
| 24–30 | Everything ripples into the end card (the style frame); final chord at 28, the frame holds, a few tiles twinkle |

Sound: synthwave score made in code (kick, clap, hats, octave bass, detuned pad, square arpeggio),
card-flip clicks, a thump and pop for each letter, a whoosh on each wave. Mastered to −14 LUFS.
